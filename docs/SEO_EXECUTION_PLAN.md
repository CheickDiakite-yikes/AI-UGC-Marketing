# Predi AI SEO Execution Plan (2 Weeks)

This plan turns the SEO strategy into shippable work. It focuses on technical SEO, landing pages, and measurement so we can scale content and ads next.

## Week 1 - Technical SEO + Core Landing Pages
Day 1-2
- Launch canonical public routes for About, How It Works, Showcase, Privacy, Terms.
- Add SEO landing pages for high-intent keywords (AI marketing platform, AI video ads, marketing for small business, long-form AI video).
- Wire metadata, Open Graph, Twitter, and JSON-LD FAQ for each landing page.

Day 3-4
- Expand sitemap coverage and block auth-only routes in robots.
- Add internal links from the home footer to new landing pages.
- Validate structured data (Rich Results Test) and indexing in GSC.

Day 5
- Create a keyword rank baseline and indexation report template.
- Define UTM taxonomy for ads and organic attribution.

## Week 2 - Content Cluster + Authority
Day 6-8
- Draft one pillar page per hub (AI marketing OS, AI video, brand consistency, ROI/credits).
- Add FAQ expansions and internal links between hub pages and landing pages.

Day 9-10
- Publish 4 supporting posts (one per hub) and link back to the core landing pages.
- Add a structured "How it works" FAQ section on each hub page.

Day 11-12
- Seed 5-10 initial backlinks (directories, founder profiles, partner communities).
- Schedule user showcases and attribution blurbs for trust signals.

Day 13-14
- Review top 10 keywords for CTR improvements and refine titles/descriptions.
- Finalize a weekly reporting dashboard (GSC + analytics + conversion to trial).

## Done Criteria
- All public pages are indexable, canonicalized, and in the sitemap.
- Four landing pages ship with schema and internal links.
- Baseline reporting exists for indexation, CTR, and conversions.
