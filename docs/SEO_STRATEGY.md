# Predi AI SEO Strategy (Forensic Level)

This plan prioritizes organic growth for high-intent "marketing tool" searches and AI discovery. It is organized by foundation, content, authority, and measurement.
Execution checklist: see `docs/SEO_EXECUTION_PLAN.md`.

## 1) Technical SEO Foundation
- Indexing:
  - Confirm clean sitemap coverage (root, About, How It Works, Showcase).
  - Add canonical tags for all public pages.
  - Ensure robots allow crawling for public routes and block auth-only routes.
- Performance:
  - Lighthouse targets: LCP < 2.5s, CLS < 0.1, INP < 200ms.
  - Preload hero fonts and compress media.
- Metadata:
  - Unique titles and descriptions per page.
  - Open Graph + Twitter metadata for sharing.
- Structured Data:
  - Organization, Product, FAQ, and Review schema where relevant.
  - Add VideoObject schema for showcase videos.
- URL Hygiene:
  - Short, descriptive slugs for all public pages.
  - Avoid query-string versions of public content.
- Accessibility:
  - Alt text and semantic headings aligned with keyword clusters.

## 2) Keyword and Intent Map
Priority buckets to target:
- Core: "AI marketing platform", "marketing automation AI", "AI marketing OS".
- Video: "AI video marketing", "AI ad creator", "UGC generator AI".
- SMB/Startup: "marketing for small business", "no marketing team tools".
- Outcome: "generate ads fast", "campaign creation tool", "reduce marketing costs".
- Long-form: "AI long video", "storyboard generator", "video continuity".

## 3) Content Strategy (Topic Clusters)
Create durable, authoritative hubs:
- Hub 1: AI Marketing OS
  - What is an AI marketing OS
  - AI marketing vs automation tools
  - How to build campaigns without a marketing team
- Hub 2: AI Video for Marketing
  - UGC video generation
  - Long-form video continuity
  - Storyboard-driven AI video
- Hub 3: Brand Consistency
  - Reference images and character consistency
  - Brand guardrails for AI generation
- Hub 4: ROI and Cost Control
  - Cost of agency vs AI
  - Credit economics explained

Each hub should link to product pages (About, How It Works, Showcase) with contextual anchors.

## 4) Showcase as SEO Flywheel
- Add a public Showcase index with categories (UGC, product, B2B, SaaS, ecommerce).
- Every showcase item should have:
  - Unique title and description.
  - Optional attribution (brand or category tags).
  - A short "How it was made" prompt summary.
- Goal: rank for "AI marketing examples" and "AI ad examples".

## 5) AI Search Optimization
AI results favor clarity and authority. Do this:
- Build a concise "What we do" section on each public page.
- Add FAQs with direct, factual answers.
- Use consistent terminology: "AI marketing OS", "brand-grounded generation", "reference-based continuity".
- Publish transparent pricing/credits explanation.
- Include founder bios and company facts (signal credibility).

## 6) Link Building and Authority
- Seed 20-30 high-quality backlinks via:
  - Founder-driven podcasts and startup directories.
  - Partnerships with marketing communities.
  - Showcase collaborations with real users.
- Publish a quarterly "AI marketing benchmarks" report to attract links.

## 7) Google Ads and Meta Ads Alignment
- Build landing pages by intent:
  - "AI marketing for small business"
  - "AI video ads generator"
  - "Long form AI video"
- Map ad groups to exact landing pages and copy the same claims.
- UTM taxonomy: source/medium/campaign/adset/ad.
- Track conversions for:
  - Trial start
  - First asset generated
  - Aha pack redemption

## 8) On-Page Checklist (Every Public Page)
- One H1 and clear page outline (H2/H3).
- Primary keyword in title, H1, intro paragraph.
- Secondary keywords in subheads and FAQ.
- Internal links to How It Works and Showcase.
- Final CTA with keyword-aligned copy.

## 9) Measurement and Diagnostics
- Weekly indexation report (GSC coverage + sitemap).
- Keyword rank tracking for top 20 targets.
- Page-level CTR analysis for title/description tuning.
- Conversion rate by landing page intent.
- Content decay checks every 90 days.

## 10) 30-60-90 Day Plan
30 Days:
- Implement metadata, schema, and canonical tags.
- Launch 4 hub pages and 8 supporting posts.
- Showcase categories and FAQ sections.

60 Days:
- Publish benchmarks report.
- Secure first 10 backlinks.
- Optimize top 5 pages for CTR.

90 Days:
- Expand landing pages by intent and vertical.
- Add programmatic showcases with structured tags.
- Begin content refresh cycle.
